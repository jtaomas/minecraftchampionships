# Bingo / Lockout — owner playtest feedback #1 (played with friends)

## World
1. **Everyone in the same place.** With several teams, all teams spawn together in the same world at the same start, not split up 1v1-style in separate areas.
2. **Many mini biomes inside the same border.** The seed he played was "just straight ice spikes and taiga, nothing else". Keep the world border size as it is, but fit **10–15 small biomes** inside it. Owner suggests looking for world-generation mods/datapacks online; any method is fine.
3. **Some structures** inside the border, "more useful structures around", but "don't overdo it".
4. **More caves** generated, and **a tool that helps you find caves**.

## Card
5. **Items must be obtainable in the world that was generated** (no item whose source biome/mob/structure is not inside the border).
6. **Fewer easy items.** About 10 items on his card could be crafted from wood alone — too easy.
7. **Weirder, more specific items.** The current pool is "too generic". With 10–15 biomes available, ask for specific things (e.g. an oak sapling, not any sapling).
8. **Harder items are worth more.**

## Kit and rules
9. **Tools are unbreakable.**
10. **A stack of steak** to start (nobody wants to hunt for food).
11. **PvP on** — you can kill people — with **keep inventory** and **no item drops** on death.
12. **`/top`** command (teleport up to the surface). The card's teleport-to-start stays.

## UI
13. **Left-clicking the bingo card item inside the inventory opens the card**, as well as right-clicking it in hand.

## Standing instruction from the owner (all games)
Our own designs come out too plain; copy what good community work does much more directly (e.g. item pools from well-regarded public bingo datapacks/plugins), respecting any stated licence.
