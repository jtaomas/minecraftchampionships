# Battle Box v3 — Additional Arena Archetypes (Research)

Purpose: structural archetypes and gameplay principles for 3+ new original Battle Box arena themes, complementing the existing 3 themes (`greenhouse`, `toyworkshop`, `skilodge`). No official layouts or names are copied.

## Archetypes Studied (2026-11-24, web search)

1. **Canal / Harbour Vessels (Maritime Crossing)**
   - **Structure:** A shallow water canal/basin dividing the arena, with moored vessels/barges and dockside walkways along the flanks.
   - **Play:** Wide sightlines across open water/decks favor ranged pressure, while below-deck/dockside cover and gangplanks create contested crossing points.
   - **Our original take (`canalships` — Harbour Canal):** A central stone-lined canal (`y = -1` water, `y = -2` bed) with docked cargo barges on each half, a central timber/stone footbridge leading to the dais, and a dockside crane/warehouse flank.

2. **Rail / Train Station (Parallel Carriages & Open Concourse)**
   - **Structure:** Parallel train carriages/bogeys running along the flanks with multiple side doors, flanking an exposed central concourse/dais.
   - **Play:** Close-quarters weaving inside carriages vs. long-range pressure from platforms; direct rushing is punished unless coordinated.
   - **Our original take (`trainstation` — Grand Terminus):** A brick-and-iron terminus hall with parallel passenger carriages on the flanks (walk-through interiors with side doors and roof access), a central ticket-booth/clock-pillar divider, and an open concourse around the dais.

3. **Villa / Courtyard (Side-Exit Architecture & Garden Flanks)**
   - **Structure:** Spawn buildings with side-tucked exits rather than a straight front door, opening into structured garden/courtyard walls and a raised colonnade/terrace.
   - **Play:** Breaks instant spawn-to-spawn sightlines, forces deliberate flank pushes through garden arches, and rewards mid-level colonnade control.
   - **Our original take (`villacourtyard` — Sunlit Villa):** Terracotta-and-sandstone villa with side-exit loggias (no front door), a central sunken/framed courtyard with cypress hedges and fountain cover, and a raised colonnade balcony on one flank.
