# frames/ — cited video evidence

A11 owns the video analysis (`../video_clouds2/`). This folder contains only the two
frames I actually opened and used to confirm a specific claim. They come from the
existing fan-recreation frame set at
`game-plugins/research/acerace/fan_recreations/frames/X4l2WSQv0Wk/`.

**Caveat:** the folder name says *fan recreation*. Treat these as supporting evidence
for intent and HUD, not as a pixel-exact capture of `mcc_clouds2`.

| Frame | Source | What I used it for | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `f_0034_start_line.jpg` | X4l2WSQv0Wk, 00:34 | Confirms the start/finish gate language: a green hedge arch over a black/white checkered banner, a white pen beyond it with a dark-green floor arrow, and three yellow chevron strips on the approach. Matches the world's lime-concrete hedge, black/white concrete line at z=20, white pen, and yellow-glazed chevrons. | M |
| `f_0130_finish_approach.jpg` | X4l2WSQv0Wk, 02:10 | Confirms two HUD/route claims: (a) the actionbar text **“You are in 1st place”** is what the player sees during the run, matching the command at (−51,1,29); (b) the player is in **survival-style health/hunger**, matching A12's report that the world runs in survival on the review server. The yellow pad shown does not match the download's chevron texture exactly, so it is not used for pad-art claims. | M for HUD, L for pad art |

Do not cite these for geometry that can be measured directly from the world; use the
renders in `../renders/` for that.
