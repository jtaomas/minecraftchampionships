# Palette — A6c arenas

Counts are exact source block counts from A6’s `data/blocks_by_island.csv`; shares are of that arena’s total non-air blocks. **H** (decoded block table). `barrier` and `light` are part of the totals but are invisible in renders; where they dominate, the “visible build” is much smaller than the total. See `other_arenas.csv` for total/barrier/renderable.

## Cross-arena notes

- Team colour is consistent: red/orange/yellow/lime/blue concrete + glass + terracotta. **H.**
- `barrier` dominates Sky Battle and Sky Race; do not compare total block counts without subtracting it. **H.**
- `light` is 38.9% of Super Clash and is invisible; the renderable build is 10,040 blocks. **H.**
- No resource pack is loaded in these renders; copper/gold colours are the renderer’s approximations. **H.**
