## Not cited as evidence

- `renders/start_iso.png`, `renders/start_top.png`: early crops that show the roof over the start; useful only as context, superseded by `start_room_iso.png` / `frames/start_box_detail.png`.
- `renders/elev_z_y.png`, `renders/elev_x_y.png`: facade elevations; they show the roof and curtain silhouette but are less informative than the profile.
- `renders/reach_all.png`, `renders/reach_lower.png`: the reachability probes; they confirm the lower deck is isolated without bridging (only z8..24 reachable with a ≤4 jump), but the images are analysis aids, not design references.
- `renders/plan_*.png`, `renders/layer_y-*.png` other than y=-35: intermediate scans.

## Confidence

All images are H evidence for geometry, palette and the positions of blocks/entities. Interpretation ("decorative", "intended", "fast") is M/L and labelled in the captions. The chickens are entities and are **not** drawn by `render_preview.py`; the finish images show the basin, not the 24 chickens. The number/positions of chickens come from `data/entities.csv`.
