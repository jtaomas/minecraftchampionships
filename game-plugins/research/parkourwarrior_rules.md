# Parkour Warrior (MC Championship / MCC Island): rules research

Prepared 2026-11-24 for a private, non-commercial fan-server plugin. Everything here is paraphrased. Each fact has a confidence:
**H** = stated directly by an official Noxcrew rules page, or by two or more sources; **M** = one wiki source, or seen once in video; **L** = inferred or uncertain.

## Correction to the current plugin

The current plugin has three easy/medium/hard lanes side by side **in every stage**. No version of the game works like that.
- **Original (S1):** one linear course and nothing else. It has no difficulty lanes.
- **Redesigned (S2, current):** one **easy main line** runs through 5 stages. At the start of each stage, one optional **bonus detour** branches off. Only the **very end** of the course splits three ways (Easy, Medium or Hard ending), and each player picks one.

So the only "three lanes" in the real game are the final ending choice. There is also one bonus branch per stage, which is optional and runs off to the side of the main line.

## Version A: Parkour Warrior (Original), MCC 1–11 (2019–2020)

Events: MCC 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 11. It was not chosen in MCC 3 or MCC 7. It was retired after MCC 11, mainly because the course was too hard (S2) **H**.

| Topic | Rule | Conf. | Src |

## Version B: Parkour Warrior (Redesign, "Season 2"), MCC 26 onward (Oct 2022 to now)

Events (S1/S4): MCC 26, 28, 30, 31, 32, 34, 35, R3, HH, BG, MU and SB. It was not chosen in R2. EC, CTW and FS ran the Survivor remix instead (see Version C). **H**

### Structure
| Topic | Rule | Conf. | Src |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main line | A single **easy main branch** made of **5 stages × 3 sections = 15 main challenges**. Each one completed gives a **stone medal**. | H | S1, S6 |
| Bonus paths | At the **beginning of each stage**, an optional bonus path branches off (5 in total). Each has **3 sections**, harder than the main line. The 1st bonus section gives **bronze**, the 2nd **silver** and the 3rd **gold**, whatever its difficulty. The maximum is 5 bronze, 5 silver and 5 gold. | H | S1, S6 |
| Leaving a bonus | You can leave a bonus path at any time with a **teleportation scroll** (hotbar item), which returns you to the main line. | H (rule) / L (exact destination) | S1 |
| Scroll removal | The scroll is taken away when **30 s** of the game remain. Chat says it was taken away because less than 30 seconds remain. In MCC 28 the cut-off was 2 min; it became 30 s from MCC 30. | H | S4, V1 4:25 |
| Endings | At the end of the main line, each player picks an **Easy, Medium or Hard** ending. From MCC 30 each ending has **2 challenges**. Before that, Easy had 1 and Medium and Hard had 3. Players may switch to a different difficulty until the 30 s mark. | H | S1, S4 |
| Stage themes | Each stage is a themed "zone". The chat shows "You completed the Industrial zone!" and "…the Candy zone!" (MCC 32). | M | V1 0:55, 1:40 |
| Look | Floating islands on a light sky. The main line runs straight ahead, and the bonus branches go off to the side. The Hard ending was a tall vertical climb (red/white tower, MCC 32). The finish is a large ornate build. | M | V1 |
| Time | **10 min**, then **30 s Overtime**. During Overtime you can still complete challenges, but **a fall ends your run**. The world's daylight goes from morning to evening over the game. | H | S1 |
| Fall (normal time) | You respawn at your last checkpoint. Chat: "You fell! Returning to last checkpoint." | H | S1, V1 0:02, 1:15, 2:45 |
| Checkpoint | Each completed section sets a checkpoint. The action bar shows "Spawn point updated", and chat shows "You completed <Section Name> and earned a stone/silver/... medal!" | M | V1 0:15, 0:55 |
| Finish broadcast | Chat broadcasts "<player> finished the entire course!" to everyone. | M | V1 3:58–4:38 |
| Play | Individual runs. Medal coins are individual, and the multiplier is shared by the team. | H | S1, S6 |
| Moving parts | **Moving blocks** exist. From MCC R3 they show indicators of where they will move. | H | S4 |
| Community courses | From MCC 32, some sections are community-made; for example, a competitor built the Medium ending in MCC BG. Courses change between events. | H | S4 |

### Scoring (current; official S6 **H**)
- **Stone medal** (main section): 2 coins each.
- **Metal medals** use a curve based on how many of each type you have:

| Count | Bronze | Silver | Gold |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 | 10 | 15 |
| 2 | 15 | 25 | 30 |
| 3 | 25 | 45 | 55 |
| 4 | 40 | 70 | 90 |
| 5 | 60 | 100 | 140 |

- **Team multiplier:** it starts at x1 (V1 sidebar shows "Team multiplier x1"), and each player who completes an ending adds to it: **Easy +0.15, Medium +0.35, Hard +0.80**. Recorded highs are x2.10–x2.65 (S1) **H**.
- **Final coins:** the team's medal coins are summed and multiplied by the team multiplier. The extra coins from the multiplier are shared out by weight: **0.40 for no ending, 0.45 Easy, 0.65 Medium, 1.10 Hard** (since MCC 35) **H** (S4).
- A perfect run is 15 stone (30) + 60 + 100 + 140 = **330 coins** before the multiplier, plus the Hard ending (V1 hotbar shows 15/5/5/5 medals) **M**.
- History (S4): MCC 26 paid flat 2/5/15/35 per medal with multipliers +0.15/+0.40/+1.00. MCC 28 introduced the count-based curve with Hard at +1.25. MCC 30 set today's values. MCC 34 introduced the weighted split.

### HUD (V1, MCC 32) **M**
- **Boss bar** at the top: a row of medal icons/dots that fill in as you progress, with **"TIME LEFT mm:ss"** under it.
- **Sidebar:** "MCC32 (6/8) PARKOUR WARRIOR", then "MEDALS OBTAINED" with the top 3 players plus your own line, "TEAM MULTIPLIER x1.3", and "MAIN CHALLENGES LEFT n/15".
- **Hotbar:** the medal items stack up as counters (stone, bronze, silver, gold), plus the teleportation scroll.
- **Feedback:** chat messages for medals and zones, an action-bar line for checkpoints, and a red chat line for falls.

## Version C: Parkour Warrior Survivor (elimination)

### C1. MCC event remix: MCC EC (Ender Cup), MCC CTW, MCC FS
| Topic | Rule | Conf. | Src |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure | A run through **9 "leaps"** of 3 obstacles each. Leap 9 is the final leap, ending at a **throne**. All leaps follow one after another on one course. | H | S3, S7 |
| Player gate | Each leap ends at a **Player Gate** that lets through only a set number of players. That cap shrinks by 5 per leap from the 2nd leap, down to a floor of 10 (5 in EC). When the cap is reached or the timer runs out, **10 s of overtime** starts. | H | S3, S7 |
| Penalty | When overtime starts, everyone still on the leap takes damage. If they still haven't finished after 10 s, they take a second hit and are **teleported to the intersection** to start the next leap. Damage starts at 1.5 hearts and rises by 0.5 per leap. In EC, damage was continuous instead and max health shrank per leap (10, 10, 9, 9, 8, 7, 5, 3, 1). Reaching 0 health eliminates you. | H | S3, S7 |
| Scoring (CTW+) | 15 per leap completed, 5 for every elimination you outlive, 30 for reaching leap 9, 100 for the sole finisher. The first five to finish a leap get +30/20/15/10/5. EC used different values: 30 per leap, 50 to the leap champion, 5 per outlived elimination, 100 to the winner. | H | S3, S7 |

### C2. MCC Island Survivor (since 0.9.0, July 2023)
8–16 players, 8 leaps. Leaps 1–7 have 3 obstacles each and last 50 s. Leap 8 is the "Final Duel" with 5 obstacles and lasts 2:30. Max health per leap: 10, 9, 8, 7, 5, 3, 1. Player limits per leap: 14, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, then the winner. Missing the limit or the time costs 1 heart every 3 s. The first player to finish a leap is the "Leap Champion": their time is announced and they get a golden glow. Nearby players are hidden so you can see your jumps. The course is generated from a pool of obstacles (with a seed). **H** (S10)

## Version D: MCC Island Parkour Warrior Dojo (since 0.8.0, June 2023): not a main-event mode
Solo and asynchronous: other players share the lobby but don't interact. Each course has **9 mandatory obstacles in a straight line**, **9 bonus obstacles** branching off from **3 frog-statue islands to the right** of the line, and **3 ending obstacles**, of which you pick one. A run is 10–19 obstacles. Each obstacle gives 1 medal; the Medium ending gives 2 and the Hard ending gives 3. Completion tiers: Standard, Advanced (16 medals, under 5 min) and Expert (21 medals, under 5 min). There are 7 monthly courses and 1 daily course, with obstacles rotating in from the community. A "Go!" title starts the run. **H** (S9)
So the Dojo is the same idea as the S2 main-event game (main line, side bonuses, a 3-way ending), as a solo time trial.

## Recommendation: which version fits "a single map, really cool, very unique"

**Build Version B (the S2 redesign, MCC 26+), dressed with Version A's themed stages.**

Why:
1. **It is still a single course.** One main line runs from start to finish through 5 themed zones. The bonus detours hang off that line, and the 3-way split appears only at the very end. That matches the owner's "single course" description and replaces the wrong lanes-in-every-stage design.
2. **It is the distinctive version.** The medals, the bronze/silver/gold detours, the teleport scroll out of a detour, the ending choice that raises the team multiplier, and 30 s of sudden-death overtime are found in no other MCC movement game. Version A is "get as far as you can on one hard course", and most players never see the second half.
3. **It is the current, documented version.** The official rules page (S6) gives exact values, the History table (S4) shows how it was tuned, and V1 shows the HUD and feedback text.
4. **It plays well at any skill level.** Everyone can finish the easy main line and Easy ending, while strong players chase gold medals and the Hard ending. Version A was retired because almost nobody could finish it.
5. **Version A's look is the "really cool" part.** Use its long chain of very different themed islands over a cloud sea (desert, garden, jungle, space, ocean, city, ice, nether), but with original builds and names only.

Survivor (C) is a different game: an elimination battle royale built from short leaps. Keep it as a possible later remix rather than the base.

### Defaults (not researched; put them in config)
- Exact obstacle distances and heights.
- Where the teleport scroll drops you: the start of the current stage's main section, or your last main-line checkpoint.
- Section count per ending: use 2, the post-MCC 30 value.
- Moving-block speed.
- Number of players per team.

## Draft acceptance checklist (S2-based build)

1. The arena is **one continuous course**: a single main line from one start platform to one finish. There are no parallel difficulty lanes inside stages.
2. The main line has **5 stages × 3 sections**. Completing each main section gives exactly **1 stone medal**, shown as a hotbar item count.
3. Each stage has **one optional bonus path** that branches off near its start and has **3 sections**. They give **bronze, silver and gold**, in that order.
4. Bonus medals can only be earned once per section per player; repeating a section gives nothing.
5. Every completed section (main or bonus) sets the player's checkpoint, with action-bar text like "Spawn point updated" and a chat line naming the section and the medal earned.
6. During the 10 minutes, falling below the course (into the void, or below a kill-height) teleports the player to their last checkpoint within about 1 s. A red "You fell! Returning to last checkpoint." line appears. No medals are lost.
7. Every player gets a **teleportation scroll**. Using it on a bonus path returns the player to the main line, and their bonus medals so far are kept.
8. The scroll is removed, with a chat notice, when **30 s** of game time remain.
9. After stage 5, the main line ends at a junction with **three ending entrances: Easy, Medium and Hard**. Each ending has 2 sections, and the difficulty visibly increases.
10. A player may leave one ending and switch to another until the 30 s mark, but no later.
11. Completing an ending adds **+0.15 / +0.35 / +0.80** to the team multiplier (starting at x1.0), once per player. It broadcasts "<player> finished the entire course!", and the player becomes a spectator or stays at the finish.
12. The game lasts **10:00**, followed by **30 s Overtime**. In Overtime, a fall **ends that player's run**: they don't respawn, and their score is frozen.
13. When Overtime ends (or all players have finished or been knocked out), coins are calculated: stone medals pay 2 each, and metal medals use the count curve (bronze 5/15/25/40/60, silver 10/25/45/70/100, gold 15/30/55/90/140).
14. Team score = sum of medal coins × team multiplier. The multiplier bonus is split by ending weight (0.40 / 0.45 / 0.65 / 1.10 for none / Easy / Medium / Hard).
15. The boss bar shows "TIME LEFT mm:ss" and counts down correctly, including Overtime.
16. The sidebar shows the game name, "Medals obtained" (top players plus self), the team multiplier, and "Main challenges left n/15", all updating live.
17. The 5 stages are visibly different themed zones. Finishing the 3rd main section of a stage announces "You completed the <Zone> zone!"
18. At least 8 distinct obstacle mechanics appear across the course, including ladder jumps, trapdoor jumps, neo (around-the-wall) jumps, ice, slime bounces, chains or iron bars, head or tight-block jumps, and at least one **moving-block** section with a visible indicator of where the blocks will be.
19. Bonus sections are noticeably harder than the main sections of the same stage, and the Hard ending is harder than any bonus section.
20. Players don't collide or block each other. Nearby players are hidden or translucent so they don't block the view of the next jump.
21. Players can't skip ahead by falling (the MCCI 0.12.1 void-respawn exploit): a respawn never puts a player past their last earned checkpoint.
22. The daylight goes from morning at the start to evening at the end. This is cosmetic.
23. No player can take damage from anything during the normal 10 minutes, and hunger and items can't be dropped or moved.
