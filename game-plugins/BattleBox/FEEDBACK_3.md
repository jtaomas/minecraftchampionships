# Battle Box — owner playtest feedback #3 (on the three-arena redesign)

## All arenas
1. **Centre blocks are in the wrong place.** They currently sit *on top of* the centre fixture; they must be *inside* it, where the "C" lanterns are (the lantern-marked centre spot). Find what the owner means in the build (the centre marker with lanterns) and put the capturable blocks in that position, not above it.
2. **Stairs/entrances blocked by a block** — found in Greenhouse (a block in the stairs at the entrance, and the way up the stairs blocked) and in Ski Lodge (right-hand side entrance: going up, you're blocked). "You need to test that you can actually go up." The route check passed these, so it is not strict enough: it must walk every staircase and every spawn exit with a real player box (0.6 wide, 1.8 tall, full headroom while stepping up), on both teams' sides.

## Ski Lodge — "100% the best designed one"
Keep the design: river, ice, lots going on, the tower at the top ("so cool, I love that"). Only fix the blocked right-hand entrance stair. Use this map as the quality bar for the other two.

## Greenhouse — the worst one
- Blocked stairs (above).
- **Remove the big box in the middle** (the central glass hall). "The whole map's already in a box; you don't need another box in the middle" — too blocky, not enough freedom of movement.
- "Decent idea but doesn't work for this game." Needs a real rethink, not a patch.

## Toy Workshop — okay, has potential
- Likes the bridge idea, but there is "not a lot of nuance": you either sit at the bottom or go up and shoot. Nothing else going on.
- Keep the bridge, build more around it: more routes, more reasons to be in different places.

## Research instruction
Look at **publicly available fan-made Battle Box maps** (community recreations and originals — not Noxcrew's own builds) for how good arenas create options: routes, height, cover, flanks, centre design. Use the ideas; original layouts and names.
