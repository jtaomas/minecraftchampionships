# Parkour Warrior — owner playtest feedback #1

Owner verdict: "looks great", "some really good ideas, but you need better execution". Loves the structure (main line with side paths branching off at hubs). Two big problems: **the main course is too easy**, and **several hard obstacles are physically impossible**. Names below are as the owner said them (match to the obstacle names in `CourseMap`).

## General
- Main course too easy overall, especially zones 1 and 2. Players know how to play Minecraft; one-block jumps are not an obstacle.
- Difficulty must **ramp up** along the course; later zones are currently not clearly harder than earlier ones.
- **More variety than plain jumps.** Use other parkour elements (owner's example: glass panes and "different weird things").
- **No easy bypass.** If an obstacle has a hard way and a trivially easy way (walking along the sides), the easy way must not exist on that obstacle. Different difficulty = different path = different points.
- **Nothing impossible.** Every jump must be proven makeable in survival before the owner sees it — by a movement simulation or bot; the owner will also test.
- **Research:** study publicly available, well-regarded parkour maps extensively: what good courses look like, which elements they use, what is possible vs impossible. Take elements from maps that are free to use (not Noxcrew's builds). Understand the hard parts and reuse the ideas.
