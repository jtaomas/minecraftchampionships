# To Get To The Other Side — owner playtest feedback #2 (Kite Festival and Frozen Canal rebuilds)

Owner verdict on both: "a lot better than before", "both of them are good, the existing structure is fine" — but **too plain**. Keep the course structure; add content.

## Both maps
- **Too plain.** Kite Festival is "big squares winding around, there's literally nothing". Frozen Canal the same. "The other maps are not as plain as that."
- **More obstacles, more design features, more interactive features.** His examples: a **swinging pendulum**, "the obvious traps in most games". Things that move and that you have to time.
- **The current parts are also too easy.** Make the hard line genuinely hard: e.g. "a **one-block gap in a massive wall**" for some of the hard openings.

## Kite Festival
- The colour-coded lines (safe / fast / hard) exist but **give no extra points**. Taking the harder path must pay: different paths give different amounts of points. "That's not even integrated yet."

## Research method (owner instruction)
The first research pass worked from text and search summaries, and it shows. This time the research must **look at images** of real courses and **use the actual maps** (download them and study the builds) so the design features are seen, not inferred: what fills the space between gates, what the obstacles and traps are, how the scenery frames the course, what moves.

## Previously identified, to fold into the same pass
- Checkpoints on Kite Festival (respawn at the last ledge passed, not the start of a 1,400-block course).
- Firework refill rings mid-flight.
- Fresh boats mid-course on Frozen Canal (boat depots).

## Later additions from the same play session (owner, in chat order)
- **Snowballs:** 25 per attempt, knockback 2.5× stronger.
- **Karma removed:** "I want to punch everyone… there's no point."
- **Dam Face:** a random hole into the void somewhere on the climb — find and close it. No redesign.
- **Decoy chickens reworked:** remove the **fake coops/pens** (Quarry Steps, Orchard Drop, anywhere else). Instead scatter decoy chickens everywhere. Decoys **walk around**, carry the **same "Punch me!" name tag**, and when punched answer **"haha lol"** (to the puncher) instead of finishing them.
- **Ticket Gates:** the anvil — owner assumes it was meant to **drop when someone jumps on it**; it does not. Make it do that. And the map is **too short**; make it longer.
