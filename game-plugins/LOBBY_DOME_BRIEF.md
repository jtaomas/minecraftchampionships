# Lobby hub + Decision Dome — reference notes and brief

The owner shared in-game screenshots of the big event's official lobby and decision dome as the **quality and scale benchmark**. These are the takeaways (scale, composition, density, mood). Match those qualities. The identity must be our own event brand, **Block Showdown** (logo: a red/blue cube with a gold top; colours navy, gold, red, team rainbow) — see `C:\Users\USER\Desktop\Mcc\resourcepack\preview_sheet.png`.

## What the benchmark looks like (observed qualities)
- **Scale**: the hub is a whole island several hundred blocks across, surrounded by ocean that fades to the horizon, with natural terrain (forest-covered hills, big grey stone mountains/cliffs with real shaping, beaches, a lagoon). It is a place, not a platform.
- **Night-time showpiece**: dark starry sky, the venue lit up like a stadium; searchlight beams (end rods / beacon-style light shafts) sweeping up from the stage; fireworks overhead; colourful hot-air balloons floating around the sky as backdrop props.
- **Hero venue**: the voting dome is a giant building (~80+ blocks wide, ~70+ blocks tall), a huge glass dome on top of a bold, colourful drum facade (red/white/gold with blue accents), with a big block-built **countdown display** on the front (digital-clock digits made of blocks; the plugin can update it), a big logo sign above the entrance, flags/pennants in team colours around the rim, a long red carpet with steps and flame braziers leading to the entrance, white/orange support pylons around the base.
- **Arrival plaza**: a large terracotta/brick plaza with an emblem mosaic in the centre and gold light points set into the floor, hedged (leaf) borders, a grand gate leading toward the venue: two tall banner towers framing a row of **hanging team-colour banners in rainbow order** (one per team), a white stone wall/stage between them, wide steps. Stone-brick retaining walls with hedges on top line the approach.
- **Side attractions** around the plaza, each a distinct mini-landmark: a glass-domed aquarium-style building, a volcano-ish rock feature, a beach area with palms, a tower, a rainbow-striped structure, giant decorative statues/sculptures (we use our own original ones), a small shrine/trophy plinth at the end of a path. Paths connect everything with curves.
- **Dome interior**: an arena bowl. Steep circular walls of dark grey/black concrete with white trim and red accents; **three tiers of spectator stands** (seats with team-colour details) ringing the inside; giant red-and-gold columns with gradient stripes (orange→yellow) rising to the ceiling; a big central column/chandelier; large logo signs hanging from a dark steel truss ceiling; the floor is a deep pit with a circular white stage. Around the centre sit the **voting pads**, one per game option, each a big chunky coloured glass/wool structure in a different team colour (rainbow ring), with a raised central podium (gold) where the result is revealed.
- **Density**: everything is detailed — gradients in walls, trims, stripes, rails, seats, signage, light rigs. Colour is bold and saturated against dark/neutral backgrounds.

## Do NOT copy
No crown on the dome, no "MCC"/"MC Championship" lettering or logos, no copies of their mascot characters or statues, no replication of their layout. Use our Block Showdown logo/brand, our own statues (e.g. giant trophy, a giant emblem cube, an original mascot of your design), our own topper on the dome (e.g. a giant golden trophy, a ring of beacons, a floating emblem cube).
